DialaGO — prototype carousel images
====================================

Put EXACTLY these four files in THIS folder (same folder as this file):

  1.JPEG
  2.JPEG
  3.JPEG
  4.JPEG

Full path on your machine (replace <PROJECT_ROOT> with your repo folder):

  <PROJECT_ROOT>/public/prototypes/1.JPEG
  <PROJECT_ROOT>/public/prototypes/2.JPEG
  <PROJECT_ROOT>/public/prototypes/3.JPEG
  <PROJECT_ROOT>/public/prototypes/4.JPEG

In Cursor / VS Code, open the project root, then:

  public  →  prototypes  →  (drop the four JPEGs here)

Vite serves `public/` at the site root, so the app loads them as:

  /prototypes/1.JPEG
  /prototypes/2.JPEG
  /prototypes/3.JPEG
  /prototypes/4.JPEG

If your files use lowercase ".jpg", either rename to ".JPEG" OR edit
`PROTOTYPE_IMAGE_PATHS` in `src/components/PrototypeShowcase.tsx`.
